01 / Method notes

Method note · CPS study

The denominator changes the question.

A larger below-degree share among people who found jobs can coexist with a lower entry risk among all jobseekers.

Core distinction. Entry risk asks what happens to the full linked unemployment sample. A conditional share asks what kind of job successful entrants obtained.

What the existing analysis shows

In the main adjusted models, the below-BA entry slope is −1.47 percentage points for U.S.-born respondents and −1.79 for foreign-born respondents per doubling of 1 + elapsed weeks. Among resolved reemployment events, the corresponding below-BA-share slopes are +0.85 and +0.39 points.

The birth-group interaction for the main entry-risk outcome has p = 0.142. It does not establish different duration slopes at the nominal 5% level.

See the probability relationship

If every new job is classified, P(below-BA entry) = P(any job entry) × P(below-BA | job entry). With unresolved occupations, the exact version for classified events is P(resolved below-BA entry) = P(resolved job entry) × P(below-BA | resolved job entry).

As jobfinding falls, a conditional below-BA share can rise without increasing the unconditional below-BA entry risk. This relationship does not identify why either component changed.

What this changes in interpretation

The observed transition cannot establish that a worker lowered an occupational acceptance threshold. Offer availability, selection into continued unemployment, and selection into reemployment remain possible explanations.

Evidence: duration patterns, adjusted estimates, and interpretation. Analysis dated 8 October 2026; note compiled 1 November 2026.

Measurement note · AIOE study

Exposure is only the first measurement.

An occupational index locates potential technological relevance. Outcomes and mechanisms require additional observations.

Core distinction. AIOE measures occupational overlap with AI capabilities. Its score does not determine whether technology substitutes for or complements workers.

What the existing analysis establishes

The public study reports lower average exposure among immigrant workers using its historical CPS sample and coarse mapping. The descriptive comparison is a baseline for asking which workers may be affected.

What would be needed next

A claim about displacement requires observed employment outcomes and a credible comparison design. A claim about complementarity requires evidence about the relevant tasks, productivity, or earnings; an exposure score alone is insufficient.

Why a successful index replication does not settle the worker comparison

The original project reports a 0.9949 replication correlation for the detailed occupation-level index. The CPS merge uses a coarser two-digit assignment. Agreement in the index reconstruction does not remove measurement uncertainty in the later worker mapping.

Evidence: reported results, measurement limits, and the original study. This is an explanation of the existing project, not a new literature review.

02 / Concept library

Definitions with a research use.

Each entry explains the term, why it affects interpretation, an example, and its limits.

6 of 6 concepts.

Occupational AI exposure

Definition. An index of how occupational ability requirements overlap with AI capabilities.

Why it matters. A measure of technological relevance is a starting point for a labor-market hypothesis.

In this portfolio. The AIOE project compares exposure by nativity in CPS 2017–2019.

Scope. Exposure alone does not establish replacement, complementarity, adoption, or job loss.

Source: AIOE study approach · measurement note · related question.

Entry risk and conditional share

Definition. Entry risk uses all eligible linked jobseekers as the denominator; a conditional share uses a subset who entered employment.

Why it matters. Changing the denominator changes the estimand and the interpretation.

In this portfolio. The CPS study separately reports below-BA entry risk and the below-BA share among resolved reemployment events.

Scope. A conditional share cannot be substituted for the risk among all jobseekers.

Source: duration patterns · denominator note.

Competing transitions

Definition. Mutually exclusive outcomes a person can enter from the same baseline state.

Why it matters. Employment, continued unemployment, and labor-force exit should be considered together.

In this portfolio. The CPS model retains five next-month outcomes, including employment with unresolved occupation classification.

Scope. A one-month transition model is not a cumulative-incidence estimate over a complete unemployment spell.

Source: multinomial model · related note.

Occupation crosswalk

Definition. A bridge connecting codes from different occupational classification systems.

Why it matters. A mapping can connect one source occupation to several destinations with different education thresholds.

In this portfolio. The CPS project resolves a BA threshold only when all mapped destinations agree; mixed cases remain unresolved.

Scope. A fixed 2025 education classification does not establish each historical job’s actual requirements.

Source: occupation mapping method · published crosswalk CSV.

Selection into observed employment

Definition. Workers who find jobs can differ systematically from those who remain unemployed or leave the labor force.

Why it matters. Patterns among successful jobseekers may reflect who is observed as employed.

In this portfolio. The CPS study distinguishes all-jobseeker risks from estimates conditional on resolved reemployment.

Scope. Observed duration associations do not isolate changing preferences or offer acceptance.

Source: interpretation limits · acceptance vs. offers question.

Interaction and uncertainty

Definition. An interaction estimates how one relationship varies with another characteristic.

Why it matters. Two estimated slopes may differ numerically without establishing a precisely measured group difference.

In this portfolio. The CPS below-BA entry slope difference is −0.32 points, with 95% interval [−0.75, +0.11] and p = 0.142.

Scope. A nonsignificant difference does not prove identical relationships; inference also depends on the model and variance estimator.

Source: main adjusted estimates · denominator note.

03 / Open research questions

Where the evidence points next.

Open question · Not tested here

Separate changes in acceptance from changes in offers.

Question. When unemployment lasts longer, do workers lower their acceptable job-match threshold, face a different offer distribution, or both?

Motivation. Realized transitions alone do not reveal either the offers a worker rejected or their reservation standards.

Possible approach. Combine a search model with observations on job offers, rejected offers, search behavior, and prior occupations. State explicitly which mechanism the available observations can distinguish.

What would count as progress. Show what additional observations or restrictions separate acceptance behavior from offer arrival and composition.

Current boundary. The published CPS analysis does not observe these acceptance thresholds and does not identify this mechanism.

Comes from: CPS interpretation and next steps · method note · selection concept.

Open question · Not tested here

Connect AI exposure to realized labor-market outcomes.

Question. Under what conditions does occupational exposure accompany changes in tasks, earnings, or employment, and do those patterns differ by nativity?

Motivation. The historical exposure gap does not reveal the direction of AI’s effect.

Possible approach. Improve occupational mapping, consider the index vintage, and relate exposure to observed outcomes using a clearly justified timing and comparison strategy.

What would count as progress. Distinguish exposure measurement from evidence of substitution or complementarity, while testing the assumptions required for a causal claim.

Current boundary. Neither high exposure nor a favorable wage association alone establishes complementarity or a preferred policy.

Comes from: AIOE measurement limits · measurement note · exposure concept.
