## Projects

### [Graduate Unemployment & the Jobs That Follow](https://minjtheninja.github.io/cps-duration/)
Does longer unemployment predict next-month entry into an occupation that typically requires less than a bachelor’s degree? Does the relationship differ by birthplace?  
Data: Monthly CPS 2011–2025 · Census occupation crosswalks · BLS typical entry education (2025)  
Method: Adjacent-month panel · Weighted transition risks · Adjusted models · Multinomial competing risks

### [AI Exposure and Immigrant Workers in the U.S.](https://minjtheninja.github.io/ai_immigrant_labor/)
Are immigrant workers more or less exposed to AI-driven occupational displacement than native workers?  
Data: O*NET · Felten et al. (2021) · IPUMS CPS 2017–2019  
Method: AIOE replication · Descriptive analysis
