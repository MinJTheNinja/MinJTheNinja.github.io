Retrospective note

Keep entry risk separate from conditional job composition.

What the existing analysis establishes

The CPS analysis dated 8 October reports negative duration slopes for below-BA entry risk, alongside different patterns conditional on successful reemployment.

Interpretation retained

A changing share among employed respondents cannot establish increasing below-BA entry among all jobseekers, or a worker’s changing willingness to accept a mismatch.

Next step

Investigate the observations and assumptions needed to distinguish acceptance standards from offers and selection. This is a proposed extension.
