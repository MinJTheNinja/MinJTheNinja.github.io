01 / QUESTION & APPROACH

Locate exposure before interpreting outcomes.

The project reconstructs the AI Occupational Exposure index and compares exposure across nativity and education groups. It draws on the idea that workers sort into different occupational tasks.

The original page reports an index replication correlation of 0.9949 with the published Felten et al. values across 774 occupations. Its subsequent CPS assignment is coarser than that index replication.

03 / INTERPRETATION LIMITS

What the index leaves unresolved.

Substitution and complementarity

AIOE measures overlap between occupational abilities and AI capabilities. A higher score does not by itself identify whether AI replaces tasks, supports workers, changes wages, or changes employment.

Assignment detail

The original study assigns exposure using a coarse two-digit mapping because of crosswalk constraints. This limits conclusions about differences within the mapped groups.

Technology vintage

The index predates the diffusion of ChatGPT. These estimates should not be read as a current measure of generative-AI effects.

Supported: a reported exposure difference under this mapping and index vintage.

Still open: how that exposure translates into changes in tasks, earnings, or employment.
