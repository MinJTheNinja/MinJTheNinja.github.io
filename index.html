02 / Monetary Economics

Liquidity, institutions & digital money.

How do new forms of money change the reasons people hold liquid balances, and the foundations of monetary influence?

Research question · Exploratory

Digital money &
precautionary balances.

Under what conditions could CBDCs or stablecoins change the liquid balances people hold against uncertain payment needs?

Money demand · Buffer stocks · Payment frictions

Research direction & open questions

Starting point. Examine how access to liquidity and payment timing enter the choice of a precautionary balance.

Proposed approach. Compare changes in transaction frictions with changes in liquidity access and the risks attached to the monetary instrument.

Open question. When would easier payments lower desired balances, and when might other costs or risks offset that effect?

Stage. Question formation. No model result or empirical estimate is presented here.

Research question · Exploratory

Digital currencies &
the monetary order.

When could innovation in payments change international monetary influence, and what role would financial institutions play?

CBDCs · Stablecoins · Monetary institutions

Research direction & open questions

Starting point. Separate payment convenience from the other services a currency and its financial system provide.

Proposed approach. Develop a framework that distinguishes payment use, demand for liquid or safe assets, and institutional credibility.

Open question. Under which assumptions would a change in payment technology also change currency choice or cross-border monetary influence?

Stage. Question formation. Theoretical and empirical work remain to be developed.
